SleepAud Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SleepAud, created by BoldKiteLabs ("we", "our", or "the app"), is committed to protecting user privacy. SleepAud is designed from the ground up as a private, distraction-free sleep podcast player.

1. Information Collection & Usage

SleepAud does not collect, record, or store personal data on any server operated by us.

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use SleepAud.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: The app does not include analytics SDKs (such as Firebase or Google Analytics), telemetry, or behavioral tracking.
  • No Advertising Trackers: The application contains no advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking identifiers, or Google Advertising IDs (AAID).

2. Device Storage & Local Data

All user data within SleepAud is stored strictly locally on your Android device:

  • Podcast Subscriptions & Preferences: Saved locally in your device's private app sandbox (SharedPreferences).
  • Audio Cache: Pre-downloaded and cached audio files are stored in your device's internal app cache directory and can be deleted at any time via the "Clear Cache" button within the app.
  • No Cloud Sync: Subscriptions and playback history never leave your device.

3. Third-Party Network Requests & External Feeds

SleepAud connects exclusively via encrypted HTTPS directly to third-party endpoints in response to user actions:

  • Apple iTunes Search API: When searching for podcasts, your search terms are sent directly to Apple's iTunes Search API (https://itunes.apple.com/search) to fetch results.
  • Podcast RSS Hosts & CDNs: When subscribing, refreshing feeds, or playing/downloading episodes, your device connects directly to the respective podcast publisher's RSS hosting server or audio CDN.
  • Technical Connection Information: Connecting directly to Apple or podcast hosts transmits standard HTTP request headers, including your device's IP address and the app's User-Agent string (SleepAud/1.0). We do not operate proxy servers, and we never collect, intercept, or log this traffic. These services are governed by their own independent privacy policies.

4. Device Permissions

SleepAud requests only the minimal permissions required for audio playback:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Fetches podcast RSS feeds, search results, and audio files over HTTPS.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Verifies active internet connectivity before starting network streams.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Keeps audio playback active while your screen is off or while you sleep.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Ensures CPU stays awake for audio decoding when the display powers down.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Displays playback controls (Play, Pause, Skip, Track Title) in the system notification drawer and lock screen.

5. Children's Privacy

SleepAud does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Third-party podcast feeds accessible through search or custom RSS URLs may contain explicit material, so the application is targeted at users aged 13 and older.

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be published here with an updated revision date.
